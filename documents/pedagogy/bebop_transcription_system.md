# Bebop Transcription System

**Status**: Authoritative source
**Referenced by**: [documents/llm_prompting_policy.md](../llm_prompting_policy.md), [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md)

> **Purpose**: Pedagogical philosophy and transcription rules for bebop counterpoint etudes.

---

## Overview

This repository is not a collection of "transcriptions" in the traditional sense. It is a **didactic system for internalizing bebop harmony, motion, and counterpoint** through minimal, repeatable musical structures.

The goal is not to memorize chords. The goal is to **internalize movement**.

---

## Core Philosophy

Bebop is not fundamentally about scales or chord symbols. It is about:

- **Forward motion**
- **Voice leading**
- **Resolution and tension**
- **Inevitable harmonic flow**

This system reduces each tune to a **compact, playable, and memorizable form** that encodes:

- Melodic language (treble clef)
- Harmonic identity (half-time bass)
- Continuous motion (walking bass)

Each transcription is therefore a **complete harmonic etude**, not just a lead sheet.

---

## Structure of Each Etude

Each etude is written as a **grand staff score** consisting of:

### Treble Clef (Right Hand / Lead)

- Verbatim melody transcription
- Preserves phrasing, articulation intent, rhythmic placement
- Includes chord symbols

This is the **authoritative melodic layer**.

### Bass Clef (Left Hand)

Two distinct, canonical representations:

#### Stage 1: Half-Time Bass (Harmonic Definition)

**Purpose**: Define and encode the harmony as motion, not as static chords.

**Format**:
- 2 notes per bar
- Beat 1 → position (root or 3rd)
- Beat 3 → movement toward next chord

**Key Principles**:
- Every note must contribute to **forward motion**
- Beat 3 should resolve by step into the next bar, or outline the next chord's 3rd or 7th
- Prioritize minimal motion, stepwise resolution, clear harmonic pull

**This layer answers**: "Where am I, and where am I going next?"

#### Stage 2: Walking Bass (Continuous Motion)

**Purpose**: Expand the half-time skeleton into a **fully swinging, idiomatic bebop bass line**.

**Format**:
- 4 quarter notes per bar
- Beats 1 and 3 are **identical to Stage 1 anchors**
- Beats 2 and 4 are **connective motion**

**Key Principles**:
- Do not reinvent the line—**expand the skeleton**
- Use stepwise motion (default), chromatic approach tones (especially beat 4), occasional arpeggiation
- Maximize half-step motion, enclosures, voice leading continuity

**This layer answers**: "How does the harmony *flow continuously*?"

---

## Counterpoint Philosophy

This system produces **functional, idiomatic counterpoint** without explicitly "composing" it.

### Rules

- Bass = structural, directional, grounded
- Melody = expressive, rhythmic, angular

### Key Constraints

- Avoid large leaps in both voices simultaneously
- Prefer **contrary motion**
- Ensure strong harmonic agreement on downbeats
- Use bass to **clarify**, not compete

---

## The Central Concept: Movement Over Chords

Traditional learning:
> chord → scale → lick

This system:
> motion → resolution → inevitability

We are encoding:

- Guide tone motion (3rds and 7ths)
- Chromatic approach logic
- Harmonic gravity

A correct transcription should allow the player to:

- Hear the next chord before it arrives
- Reconstruct harmony without a chart
- Generate bass lines and solos naturally

---

## Practice Method

For each tune:

### Step 1: Melody Only
- Play and memorize the melody
- Focus on phrasing and swing

### Step 2: Add Half-Time Bass
- Internalize harmonic motion
- Sing bass separately if needed

### Step 3: Loop Small Sections
- 2–4 bars at a time
- Ensure movement feels inevitable

### Step 4: Add Walking Bass
- Keep anchors intact
- Fill with stepwise and chromatic motion

### Step 5: Remove the Page
- Play from memory
- Test harmonic awareness

---

## Success Criteria

A tune is "learned" when you can:

- Play melody + bass without reference
- Sing the bass line independently
- Anticipate harmonic movement
- Improvise over the form without thinking in chord symbols

---

## Why This Works

This system mirrors how bebop musicians actually think:

- Not in static chords
- Not in scale boxes
- But in **continuous voice-led motion**

It compresses the harmonic language of bebop into a **minimal, reusable form**.

---

## Long-Term Objective

After multiple tunes:

- Harmonic movement becomes intuitive
- Walking bass emerges automatically
- Melodic phrasing aligns with harmony
- Improvisation becomes **predictive, not reactive**

---

## Summary

Each transcription is:

- A melody (what is said)
- A harmonic skeleton (why it works)
- A motion engine (how it moves)

Together, they form a **complete, internalizable model of bebop language**.
